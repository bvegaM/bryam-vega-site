abstract: >
  Most AIOps platforms are designed around a flawed assumption: every incident is a software bug waiting to be patched.

  What normally happens?

  An alert is triggered, an automated fix is generated, and the system moves on. But production environments are rarely that simple. Many incidents are not caused by defective code. Some stem from architectural limitations that no quick patch can resolve. Others originate from business processes, operational decisions, or external dependencies that engineering teams cannot fix at all.
  Treating every incident as a coding problem creates a dangerous illusion of resolution: the patch is deployed, the alert disappears, but the underlying issue remains.

  We took a different approach.

  Built on Amazon Bedrock, our AI agent starts by understanding the nature of the problem before taking action. It gathers evidence across systems, evaluates multiple hypotheses, validates them against observed behavior, and determines the true root cause. Only then does it decide the appropriate course of action: generate a code fix, identify an architectural weakness, or escalate the issue to the team best positioned to resolve it.

  The result is an AIOps system that doesn't just automate remediation—it automates reasoning.
  Less firefighting. Better decisions. Faster resolution of the problems that actually matter.
